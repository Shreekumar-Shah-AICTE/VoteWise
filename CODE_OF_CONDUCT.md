# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

VoteWise is committed to providing a non-partisan, inclusive, and accessible election education experience for all Indian citizens, regardless of caste, religion, gender, language, or political affiliation.

## Standards

- **Non-Partisan**: All content is factual and does not favor any political party or candidate
- **Inclusive**: Content is accessible in 8 Indian languages and compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA
- **Accurate**: All election information is sourced from the Election Commission of India
- **Respectful**: The platform encourages democratic participation without intimidation

## Scope

This code applies to all content within the VoteWise platform, including AI-generated responses, knowledge base entries, and quiz questions.
